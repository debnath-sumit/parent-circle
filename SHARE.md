# ParentCircle

**The village every parent actually needs.**
👉 Try it: **https://parent-circle.vercel.app**

---

## What is it?

ParentCircle is a small, parent-only network for the stuff raising kids
actually takes — borrowing a stroller from a neighbor, finding a play date for
Saturday, or asking the real question you can't quite Google.

No ads. No data sold. Just parents helping parents.

---

## Why you'll want it

- ♻️ **Save money. Skip the landfill.** Kids outgrow strollers, cribs, and
  toys in months. Pass them on — and pick up what you need from neighbors,
  free or cheap.
- 🙋 **Ask 100 parents at once.** Need a bassinet by Friday? A booster seat
  for grandma's house? Post *"Looking for…"* and let the community come to you.
- 💬 **Real advice, real parents.** Sleep regressions, picky eaters, first day
  of school — get tips from parents who lived it last month, not random forum
  strangers.
- 🤝 **Build your village.** Find local play dates, classes, and parents on
  the same stage as you. From newborn to teen — you're not doing this alone.

---

## A quick tour

1. **Give & Receive** 🎁 — share what your kids have outgrown, browse what
   neighbors are offering. Filter by category, condition, or age group.
2. **Looking for** 🙋 — flip the script: post what *you* need and let parents
   offer it publicly (in comments) or quietly (via DM).
3. **Community** 💬 — ask anything. Sleep, food, schools, screen time — all
   the questions you whisper at playgroup.
4. **Messages** ✉️ — chat one-on-one with another parent. Realtime, private,
   and notified in your top bar.
5. **Your kids' profile** 👶 — add their age group so the app can surface
   what's actually relevant.

---

## How to try it (90 seconds)

1. Open **https://parent-circle.vercel.app**
2. **Join free** with your email — takes 30 seconds.
3. Confirm via the verification email.
4. Add yourself to the **Profile** page, add a child or two.
5. Browse **Give & Receive**, or post your first "Looking for…" request.

That's it — no app store, no credit card, no algorithm trying to keep you
scrolling.
